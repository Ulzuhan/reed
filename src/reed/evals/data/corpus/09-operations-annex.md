# Customer Operations Annex

This annex is intentionally table-heavy. It is authoritative for customer support response
targets and overrides informal summaries in chat.

## Support response table

| Plan | First response | Status update |
|---|---:|---:|
| Standard | 8 business hours | 1 business day |
| Gold | 1 hour | 4 hours |
| Platinum | 15 minutes | 30 minutes |

The clock runs continuously for Platinum incidents. Standard and Gold clocks pause outside the
customer's contracted support window.

## Credits and escalation

When Platinum first response exceeds 15 minutes, the service credit is 20% of the monthly fee.
Credits are not automatic: the account owner records the breach in Purser within five working
days. Platinum incidents escalate to the VP of Customer Operations after the first missed status
update. Gold incidents escalate to the Customer Operations Lead after two missed updates.

The table contains service targets, not incident severity. Engineering still declares severity
using the production incident response runbook.
