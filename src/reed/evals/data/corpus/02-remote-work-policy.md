# Remote Work and Working Hours

Meridian Labs is remote-first. There are no required office days, and your
location has no bearing on promotion, pay band, or which projects you are given.
This policy sets out the few constraints that make that workable.

## Where you may be based

You must be based within the **UTC-1 to UTC+3** time zone band. This keeps every
employee inside a shared working window without anyone routinely taking calls at
midnight. Exceptions are approved jointly by the People Operations Lead and the
hiring manager, and are reviewed annually.

You must also be legally employable in your country of residence. If you plan to
move country permanently, tell People Operations at least 60 days ahead — a move
can change your employing entity, and that takes time to arrange.

## Core hours and availability

**Core hours are 11:00 to 16:00 WET (Lisbon time), Monday to Friday.** Be
reachable and be willing to take a meeting inside that window. Arrange the rest
of your day however you like, and record your usual hours in Almanac so
colleagues can see them.

Outside core hours, everything is asynchronous. There is no expectation that you
read Wardroom outside your own working hours, and none that you reply instantly
inside them: **acknowledge a direct message within four working hours**, even if
the answer itself takes longer.

Meetings longer than 30 minutes need an agenda in the invite, or the recipient
may decline without explanation. Any meeting with more than eight attendees is
recorded. Wednesdays remain free of recurring meetings.

## Working from another country

You may work from outside your country of employment for up to **60 calendar
days per calendar year**, with **no single stretch longer than 21 consecutive
days**. Longer stays create tax-residency and permanent-establishment exposure
for the company, so a stretch above 21 days requires written approval from the
People Operations Lead before you book anything.

While abroad you must remain inside the UTC-1 to UTC+3 band, and you must file a
working-abroad notice in Almanac **at least 10 working days before you travel**,
stating the country and the dates.

This is personal travel that happens to overlap with work. It is not a business
trip, and the expenses and travel policy governs what that means for costs.

## Devices and what you may open on them

Your company laptop is enrolled in Compass device management. That enrolment is
what makes it a *managed* device, and it is the line that matters here.

- On a **managed device** you may access data of any tier your role permits.
- On a **personal or otherwise unmanaged device** — your own laptop, a home
  desktop, a family machine — you may access **Tier 0 and Tier 1 data only**.
- Tier 2 data requires a managed device. **Tier 3 data may never be accessed
  from personal hardware under any circumstances.**

The tiers are defined in the security and data handling policy; read it before
you assume which one something falls into.

Wardroom and Compass may be installed on a personal phone provided the phone has
a passcode and locks automatically after no more than 10 minutes. The Tier 1
ceiling still applies to that phone.

## Your home office

New joiners get a **home-office setup budget of €800**, claimable in Purser
within the first 30 days. After that, a **€400 refresh is available every 24
months**. Use it for a desk, a chair, a monitor, lighting, or anything else that
makes the room work.

You also receive a **€40 per month stipend** towards internet and utilities, paid
with your salary and requiring no claim.

Any single item costing more than €250 remains company property, is recorded
against your name in Almanac, and is returned or bought out when you leave.
Everything cheaper is yours to keep.

## Coworking and hub desks

If you live more than 50 km from a hub, you may claim up to **€180 per month**
for a coworking space against an invoice. A membership contract longer than three
months needs People Operations approval first.

Desks in Lisbon and Tallinn are bookable in Almanac up to four weeks ahead, and a
booking is released automatically if you have not checked in by 11:00. Hub visits
are voluntary; the only travel we ask of you is the two annual onsites.

## When this policy is not enough

If the arrangement you need does not fit the shape above — a caring
responsibility, a chronic condition, a visa constraint — say so rather than
quietly working around it. Ask your manager or People Operations directly.
Exceptions are common and are recorded in Almanac so nobody has to re-litigate
them every year.
