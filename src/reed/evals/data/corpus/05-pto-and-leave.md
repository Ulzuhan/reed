# Time Off and Leave

Rest is part of the job, not a reward for finishing it. Everything below is
booked and recorded in Almanac and owned by the People Operations Lead.

## Annual leave

You have **25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year**, on top of the public
holidays observed where you live. Leave accrues monthly at **2.08 days** and is
pro-rated in your first and last year.

You may take leave from your first day. New joiners are limited to **five days
within their first 90 days**; more than that needs People Operations approval,
which is usually given when the days were already booked before you accepted.

Two days are closed company-wide and are **not** deducted from your balance: 24
December and 31 December.

## Booking and approval

Give **10 working days' notice for any absence of five days or more**, and two
working days for one or two days.

Your manager must respond in Almanac **within three working days**. If they have
not responded by then, the request is automatically approved and you should treat
it as confirmed. Managers may only decline with a reason, and may not decline
twice in a row for the same requested period.

## Carryover

Up to **five days** may be carried into the next calendar year and must be used
by **31 March**. Anything above five days, and anything not used by 31 March,
lapses. Unused leave is paid out only when you leave the company.

## The minimum

Everyone must take at least **10 days off in a calendar year**, including one
unbroken block of **five consecutive working days**. This is a floor, not a
target, and People Operations will chase anyone below it in October.

## Sick leave

You have **10 self-certified paid sick days per calendar year**. Record the
absence in Almanac the same day where you are able to, and tell your manager —
one line in Wardroom is enough, and no explanation of the illness is required.

A medical certificate is needed **from the fourth consecutive day** of absence.
Beyond 10 days in a year, statutory sick pay in your country applies and People
Operations will coordinate the arrangements with you directly.

Sick days taken during booked annual leave are converted back to sick leave with
a certificate, so a holiday spent ill is not a holiday spent.

## Parental leave

After **six months of service**, the primary caregiver receives **18 weeks of
fully paid parental leave** and the secondary caregiver **six weeks**, in both
cases topped up by Meridian Labs to 100% of salary where the statutory payment in
your country is lower.

Leave may be taken in up to **three separate blocks** within the first 18 months
after birth or placement. On returning, you work **80% of your hours at 100% of
your pay for the first four weeks**.

Tell People Operations as early as you comfortably can — we hold roles open and
backfill temporarily, and that is easier with notice.

## Other leave

**Bereavement leave** is five days for an immediate family member and two days
otherwise, not deducted from annual leave. We do not ask for proof.

**Carer's leave** is five paid days per calendar year for a dependant's illness
or a care arrangement that has fallen through.

**Moving day**: one paid day per calendar year when you relocate home.

**Public holiday swap**: you may exchange up to three of your local public
holidays for other days in the same year, recorded in Almanac at least five
working days ahead.

## Sabbatical

After **four years of continuous service** you may take up to **eight weeks of
unpaid sabbatical** with your role held open, once per four-year period. Request
it at least 90 days ahead; approval comes from your department lead and People
Operations. Annual leave continues to accrue during a sabbatical.

## Time off in lieu for on-call work

Being paged in the night is compensated with time, not just money. **Every night
on which you are paged between 22:00 and 07:00 earns half a day of time off in
lieu**, capped at one half-day per night no matter how many pages arrive.

Your Engineering Manager credits TOIL in Almanac within three working days of the
on-call week ending. TOIL **expires 90 days after it is earned**, so book it. It
is separate from, and additional to, the on-call stipend set out in the incident
response runbook.

## Leaving well

Unused annual leave is paid out at your daily rate on your final payslip;
carryover from the previous year is included, TOIL is not. Notice periods sit in
your employment contract rather than in this handbook, and People Operations will
walk you through the sequence when the time comes.
