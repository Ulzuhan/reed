# Expenses and Travel

Spend company money as if it were your own, then claim it back promptly. This
policy is owned by the Finance Partner. Claims are submitted in Purser; travel is
booked in Waypoint.

## Pre-approval

**Any single item over €75 needs your manager's approval in Purser before you buy
it.** Below €75, use your judgement and claim afterwards.

There is one exception: purchases charged to the home-office budget, coded
**HO-1**, need no pre-approval provided they stay inside the budget set by the
remote work policy. Check that figure before you order, because anything over it
comes out of your own pocket.

Separately, and regardless of price, any software or service that will handle
Tier 2 or higher data must clear a security review before purchase. A €12 monthly
subscription is not exempt from that.

## Claiming and getting paid

Submit claims in Purser **within 30 days** of the expense date, with a legible
receipt or invoice. A claim submitted more than 60 days after the expense date is
paid only with the Finance Partner's written approval.

Approved claims are paid with the **payroll run on the 25th of each month**.
Anything approved after the 18th rolls into the following month's run, so a claim
that sits in your manager's queue is a claim that waits four extra weeks.

A company card is issued to anyone whose role reliably requires more than €500 of
spend per month. Card transactions must be reconciled in Purser with receipts
attached by the **fifth working day** of the following month; an unreconciled
card is suspended at the tenth.

## Booking travel

Book all business travel through Waypoint, **at least 14 days before departure**.
Booking inside 14 days is allowed but requires a short note in Purser explaining
why, because late fares typically cost us 40% more.

Economy is the default cabin. **Premium economy is permitted where the scheduled
flight time exceeds six hours.** Business class requires written approval from
both the Finance Partner and your department lead, granted only in exceptional
circumstances.

Rail is preferred over flying wherever the door-to-door journey is under five
hours, even where the fare is higher. Meridian Labs pays the difference gladly.

## Accommodation and daily costs

Hotel caps are **€160 per night in Lisbon and Tallinn** and **€220 per night
elsewhere**, inclusive of tax. If nothing is available within the cap, book above
it and note the reason in Purser rather than spending an evening hunting.

We pay a **per diem of €55 per day within the EU and €70 per day outside it**,
covering meals and incidentals. No receipts are required for per diem, and it is
paid for every full day away from home including travel days.

Where meals are provided — most obviously at the two annual company onsites — the
per diem does not apply. Onsite days instead carry a **€20 per day incidental
allowance**.

## Ground transport

Personal car use is reimbursed at **€0.30 per kilometre**. Taxis and ride-hailing
are reimbursable up to **€40 per trip** without pre-approval; above that, the €75
rule applies as usual. Public transport is reimbursed at cost.

## Client entertainment

Meals with customers are reimbursable up to **€60 per person**, with the full
attendee list recorded in the Purser memo field. Above €60 per person, the
Finance Partner must approve in advance.

Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of a client meal and only up to **€20 per
person**.

## What we do not reimburse

- Traffic fines, penalty fares, and late-return charges on hire cars.
- In-flight internet above **€25 per flight**.
- Seat or cabin upgrades outside the six-hour rule, and loyalty-scheme purchases.
- Personal entertainment, minibar, and spa charges.
- **Travel taken for personal reasons, including working from another country
  under the remote work policy.** Those trips are not business travel: flights,
  accommodation, and per diem are entirely at your own expense, and the fact that
  you worked during them makes no difference.

## Learning budget

Every employee has **€1,200 per calendar year** for books, courses, certifications
and conference tickets, administered by the People Operations Lead. It does not
carry over into the next year, and it is not paid out if unused.

Learning spend follows the normal pre-approval rule: anything over €75 needs your
manager's yes in Purser first. Travel to a conference is booked and reimbursed as
business travel under this policy and does not come out of the €1,200.
